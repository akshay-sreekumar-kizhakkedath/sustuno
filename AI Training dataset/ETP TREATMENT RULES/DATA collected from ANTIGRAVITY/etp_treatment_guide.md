# Comprehensive Operational Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) & Rulebook: Textile Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP) & ZLD Systems

---

## 1. Overview of Textile Wastewater Characteristics
Textile processing effluent generated from scouring, bleaching, mercerizing, reactive dyeing, printing, and washing exhibits:
- **High pH Variations**: 8.0 to 11.5 (due to Caustic Soda & Soda Ash)
- **High Chemical Oxygen Demand (COD)**: 1,200 – 3,500 mg/L
- **High Biological Oxygen Demand (BOD₅)**: 300 – 900 mg/L (BOD/COD ratio ≈ 0.25 – 0.35)
- **High Total Dissolved Solids (TDS)**: 4,000 – 12,000 mg/L (driven by Glauber's salt NaCl / Na₂SO₄)
- **Intense Color**: 500 – 2,500 PCU (unfixed reactive, disperse, sulfur dyes)

## 2. Stage-by-Stage Operational Rules Matrix

### Stage 1: Screen & Equalization Tank
- **Rule 1.1**: Operate coarse (20mm) and fine drum (1.5mm) screens 24/7 to trap yarn threads, fibers, and fabric lint.
- **Rule 1.2**: Maintain air diffusers operating at 1.8 m³/m²/hr in equalization basin to prevent solids deposition and anaerobic odor.
- **Rule 1.3**: Retention time must be minimum 16 hours to smooth out pH spikes and dye bath batch discharges.
- **Rule 1.4**: If raw feed temperature exceeds 42°C, auto-divert feed through the cooling tower.

### Stage 2: Coagulation, Flocculation & Primary Clarifier
- **Rule 2.1 (Flash Mixing)**: Dose Alum (250 mg/L) or PAC (150 mg/L) into flash mixer running at 200 RPM for 2 minutes.
- **Rule 2.2 (pH Control)**: Adjust pH to 6.8 – 7.2 for Alum/PAC coagulation using H₂SO₄.
- **Rule 2.3 (Flocculation)**: Dose Anionic Polyelectrolyte (1.0 ppm) into paddle flocculator running at 25 RPM for 20 minutes.
- **Rule 2.4 (Sludge Removal)**: Purge primary sludge to sludge holding tank every 4 hours to prevent septic gas floating.

### Stage 3: Aerobic Biological Aeration (Activated Sludge Process)
- **Rule 3.1 (Dissolved Oxygen)**: Maintain aeration basin DO between **2.0 and 3.0 mg/L** continuously.
- **Rule 3.2 (MLSS Control)**: Maintain MLSS at **3,800 ± 400 mg/L**. Calculate Sludge Volume Index (SVI) daily (target **80–120 mL/g**).
- **Rule 3.3 (Nutrients Dosing)**: Dose Urea and DAP to satisfy **BOD : N : P = 100 : 5 : 1**.
- **Rule 3.4 (RAS & WAS)**: Maintain Return Activated Sludge (RAS) at 75% of inflow. Waste sludge (WAS) daily based on MCRT target of 12 days.

### Stage 4: Advanced Oxidation Process (Fenton) & Tertiary Filtration
- **Rule 4.1 (Fenton Oxidation)**: Adjust reaction tank pH to **3.2 ± 0.2** with sulfuric acid. Dose FeSO₄ (400 mg/L) and H₂O₂ 35% (800 mg/L). Reaction time: 90 mins.
- **Rule 4.2 (Fenton Neutralization)**: Dose Lime to pH **8.0** to precipitate iron sludge in Fenton Clarifier.
- **Rule 4.3 (Filtration)**: Pass supernatant through Dual Media Filter (DMF) and Activated Carbon Filter (ACF). Backwash daily at 0.8 bar differential pressure.

### Stage 5: UF, RO & Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD)
- **Rule 5.1 (Ultrafiltration)**: Maintain UF flux at 60 LMH. Perform Air-scour Backwash every 30 minutes. Ensure SDI₁₅ < 3.0.
- **Rule 5.2 (RO Protection)**: Dose Antiscalant (4.0 ppm) and SMBS (3.0 ppm). ORP interlock set at < 200 mV.
- **Rule 5.3 (RO Recovery)**: Stage 1 RO recovery = 75%; Stage 2 High Pressure RO recovery = 70%. Combined RO water recovery = **93%**. Permeate TDS < 80 mg/L recycled to dye house.
- **Rule 5.4 (MEE & ATFD)**: Feed 7% RO reject brine to 4-Effect MEE. Condensate water recycled (TDS < 50 mg/L). Salt slurry converted to dry powder via ATFD for reuse/landfill.

## 4. Operational Diagnostics Quick-Reference

1. **Symptom**: Brown Foaming in Aeration Basin → **Action**: Check DO level (if < 1.5 mg/L, increase blowers); spray water / dose silicone defoamer 5 ppm.
2. **Symptom**: Sludge Bulking (SVI > 180 mL/g) → **Action**: Dose H₂O₂ 15 ppm into RAS line; dose Urea/DAP to fix N/P deficiency.
3. **Symptom**: RO Permeate Conductivity Rising (> 200 µS/cm) → **Action**: Perform Citric Acid CIP (2%) for scale or NaOH CIP (0.1%) for bio-fouling.
4. **Symptom**: High Fenton Sludge → **Action**: Optimize H₂O₂/FeSO₄ ratio via daily bench-scale jar test.
