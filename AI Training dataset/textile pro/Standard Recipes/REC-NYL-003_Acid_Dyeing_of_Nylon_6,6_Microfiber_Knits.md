# Standard Dyeing Recipe: REC-NYL-003 - Acid Dyeing of Nylon 6,6 Microfiber Knits

## 1. Basic Information
- **Recipe ID**: REC-NYL-003
- **Recipe Name**: Acid Dyeing of Nylon 6,6 Microfiber Knits
- **Fabric Type**: 85% Nylon 6,6 / 15% Spandex Warp Knit
- **Fabric GSM**: 210 GSM
- **Fabric Weight**: 200 kg
- **Shade Name**: Crimson Red
- **Shade Depth**: Medium-Dark (2.5% owf)
- **End Use**: Swimwear & Athletic Compression Wear

---

## 2. Dye Information
- **Dye Type**: Half-milling Acid Dye / 1:2 Metal Complex Dye
- **Dye Name**: Acid Red 138 (2.0% owf) + Acid Orange 67 (0.5% owf)
- **Dye Concentration**: 2.5% owf total dye solids
- **Dye Combination**: Acid Binary Color Combination
- **Dye Manufacturer**: Huntsman Erionyl / Archroma Telon

---

## 3. Chemical Information
| Chemical Name | Function | Dosage | Addition Sequence | Compatibility | Incompatible Chemicals |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ammonium Sulfate (NH4)2SO4 (pH Buffer)** | Slow acid releaser; gradually lowers bath pH during heating to control strike rate | 2.0 g/L | Added at start of bath filling at 40°C | Compatible with anionic leveling agents | Alkalis |
| **Anionic Retarding / Leveling Agent** | Competes for amine end-groups on Nylon to prevent streaky barre defects | 1.5 g/L | Dosed 10 min prior to dye addition at 40°C | Compatible with acid dyes | Cationic softeners |
| **Syntan After-treating Fixing Agent** | Crosslinks dye molecules inside polyamide matrix to boost wash fastness | 3.0% owf | Applied in a fresh post-treatment bath at 70°C for 20 min at pH 4.5 | Anionic compatibility | Non-ionic surfactants |

---

## 4. Process Parameters
- **Temperature Profile**: Start 40°C -> Heat to 98°C @ 1.0°C/min -> Hold 98°C for 45 min -> Cool to 70°C @ 1.5°C/min -> Syntan Fix @ 70°C
- **Heating Rate**: 1.0 °C/min (controlled rate above 70°C where dye uptake accelerates)
- **Cooling Rate**: 1.5 °C/min
- **Time**: Total Duration: 165 minutes (2.75 hours)
- **pH**: Dyeing pH: 4.8 - 5.2 (Ammonium Sulfate) | Fixing pH: 4.5 (Acetic Acid)
- **Liquor Ratio**: 1:10 MLR
- **Pressure**: 1.0 bar (Atmospheric)

---

## 5. Process Steps
- **Pretreatment**: Scoured at 70°C with non-ionic detergent; heat-set at 185°C to stabilize Spandex width
- **Dye Addition**: Dissolve Acid dyes in boiling water, dilute to 50°C, dose into vessel over 15 min
- **Salt Addition**: Glauber's salt (10 g/L) added as retarding agent to slow initial dye strike
- **Alkali Addition**: N/A (Acid pH environment mandatory)
- **Washing**: Drain dye bath at 70°C -> Cold rinse for 10 min
- **Soaping**: Apply Syntan After-fixing agent (3% owf) at 70°C for 20 min at pH 4.5
- **Neutralization**: Rinse in cold water; target fabric core pH 5.5
- **Drying**: Stenter dryer at 130°C under low tension to protect Spandex elasticity
- **Finishing**: Apply Fluorocarbon Water-repellent finish or Hydrophilic softener

---

- **Machine Type**: Atmospheric Soft-Flow Jet Dyeing Machine
- **Machine Capacity**: 250 kg nominal capacity
- **Recommended Machine**: Thies Soft-TRD Atmospheric Jet
- **Loading Percentage**: 80.0%

## 7. Quality Parameters
- **Shade Accuracy**: DE < 0.5 (CMC 2:1)
- **Color Fastness**: Grade 4-5
- **Wash Fastness**: Grade 4-5 (ISO 105-C06 at 50°C after Syntan fixing)
- **Rub Fastness**: Dry Rubbing: Grade 4-5 | Wet Rubbing: Grade 4
- **Light Fastness**: Grade 4-5
- **Uniformity**: Zero barre or streakiness across warp knit loops

---

## 8. Defects
- **Common Defects**: Barre Streakiness, Low Wet Fastness without Fixer, Elastane Degradation
- **Causes**: Rapid heating > 70°C, omission of Syntan fixer, excessive temp > 105°C on Spandex
- **Corrective Actions**: Use leveling agent at 98°C for 30 min migration hold; re-apply Syntan fixing agent

---

## 9. Safety
- **PPE Required**: Chemical goggles, nitrile gloves, apron
- **Hazardous Chemicals**: Glacial Acetic Acid, Syntan Fixing Agent (skin sensitizer)
- **Safety Precautions**: Avoid inhalation of acid vapors; maintain local exhaust ventilation
