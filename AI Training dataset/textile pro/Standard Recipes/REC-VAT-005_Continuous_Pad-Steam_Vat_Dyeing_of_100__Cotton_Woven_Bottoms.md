# Standard Dyeing Recipe: REC-VAT-005 - Continuous Pad-Steam Vat Dyeing of 100% Cotton Woven Bottoms

## 1. Basic Information
- **Recipe ID**: REC-VAT-005
- **Recipe Name**: Continuous Pad-Steam Vat Dyeing of 100% Cotton Woven Bottoms
- **Fabric Type**: 100% Cotton Heavy Canvas / Twill Woven
- **Fabric GSM**: 320 GSM
- **Fabric Weight**: 1200 kg (Continuous Run 3500 meters)
- **Shade Name**: Khaki Drab
- **Shade Depth**: Medium (18 g/L Vat Dye)
- **End Use**: Military Uniforms & Heavy Work Trousers

---

## 2. Dye Information
- **Dye Type**: Insoluble Anthraquinone Vat Dye Pigment
- **Dye Name**: Vat Olive Green (10 g/L) + Vat Brown 1 (5 g/L) + Vat Yellow 2 (3 g/L)
- **Dye Concentration**: 18 g/L total dye liquor concentration
- **Dye Combination**: Continuous Vat Trichromatic Pigment Combination
- **Dye Manufacturer**: Dystar Indanthren

---

## 3. Chemical Information
| Chemical Name | Function | Dosage | Addition Sequence | Compatibility | Incompatible Chemicals |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sodium Hydrosulfite (Na2S2O4) + Caustic Soda 38°Bé** | Chemical Reduction Pad liquor; converts insoluble vat pigment to soluble leuco form inside steamer | 40 g/L Hydros + 45 mL/L Caustic Soda 38°Bé | Prepared in chemical padder trough continuously metered | Must be maintained under strict reducing conditions | Air / Oxygen, acids |
| **Polymeric Migration Inhibitor** | Prevents vat dye pigment migration during intermediate drying phase | 10 g/L | Added directly to dye pigment pad liquor | Compatible with non-ionic wetting agents | Strong electrolytes |
| **Hydrogen Peroxide (35%) + Acetic Acid** | Oxidation bath; re-oxidizes soluble leuco vat dye back to insoluble pigment inside fiber | 3.0 mL/L H2O2 + 1.5 mL/L Acetic Acid | Dosed in 1st wash box after steaming at 60°C | Oxidizing conditions | Reducing agents (Hydros) |

---

## 4. Process Parameters
- **Temperature Profile**: Pad Pigment @ 25°C -> IR Pre-dry -> Hot Air Dry @ 110°C -> Chemical Pad @ 25°C -> Steamer @ 102°C (45s) -> Oxidation @ 60°C -> Soap @ 95°C
- **Heating Rate**: Instantaneous Steam Injection
- **Cooling Rate**: Continuous Wash Box Counter-flow Cooling
- **Time**: Fabric Speed: 40 m/min | Steamer Dwell Time: 45 seconds
- **pH**: Reduction Steaming pH: 13.0 | Oxidation pH: 4.5 - 5.5 | Final Wash pH: 6.5
- **Liquor Ratio**: Continuous Padding (65% Wet Pick-Up Expression)
- **Pressure**: Pad Mangle Nip Pressure: 3.5 bar | Steamer Pressure: Atmospheric (Positively sealed)

---

## 5. Process Steps
- **Pretreatment**: Singed, enzymatic desized, continuous bleached & mercerized woven canvas
- **Dye Addition**: Pad dye pigment liquor at 65% wet pick-up $\rightarrow$ Pass through IR pre-dryer
- **Salt Addition**: Glauber's salt (20 g/L) added to chemical reduction pad bath to suppress dye stripping
- **Alkali Addition**: Caustic Soda (45 mL/L) continuously fed to chemical pad trough
- **Washing**: Cold water overflow rinse immediately exiting steamer
- **Soaping**: Oxidize with H2O2 at 60°C $\rightarrow$ Hot soaping at 95°C in continuous 4-chamber washer
- **Neutralization**: Final wash box neutralization with Acetic Acid
- **Drying**: Continuous steam can dryer stack (24 cylinders @ 3.0 bar steam)
- **Finishing**: Apply Water-repellent / Sanforized anti-shrinkage mechanical finishing

## 7. Quality Parameters
- **Shade Accuracy**: DE < 0.4 (CMC 2:1 head-to-tail and side-to-side)
- **Color Fastness**: Grade 5 (Outstanding overall fastness)
- **Wash Fastness**: Grade 5 (ISO 105-C06 at 95°C boiled wash)
- **Rub Fastness**: Dry Rubbing: Grade 4-5 | Wet Rubbing: Grade 4
- **Light Fastness**: Grade 7-8 (Maximum light fastness for military spec)
- **Uniformity**: Zero center-to-selvedge or head-to-tail shade variation

---

## 8. Defects
- **Common Defects**: Tailing (Shade lighter at end of roll), Side-to-Center Shade Variation, Oxidation Specks
- **Causes**: High pad trough volume (>25L), non-uniform pad mangle crown nip pressure, oxygen ingress in steamer
- **Corrective Actions**: Install trough displacement wedge (renewal time <30s); adjust swimming roller pneumatic profile; seal steamer water locks

---

## 9. Safety
- **PPE Required**: Full face shield, heavy butyl rubber aprons, chemical resistant boots, respirator
- **Hazardous Chemicals**: Concentrated Caustic Soda 38°Bé, Sodium Hydrosulfite, Hydrogen Peroxide 35%
- **Safety Precautions**: Continuous toxic gas monitoring for sulfur dioxide (SO2) near steamer exit
