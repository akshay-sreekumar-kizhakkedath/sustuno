1. Exhaust Dyeing of 100% Cotton with Reactive Dyes

Substrate: 100% Cotton Knit / Woven | MLR: 1:8 | Temp: 60°C Isothermal

Component / AuxiliaryDosageFunction

Soda Ash (Na2CO3)15 g/LAlkali to induce covalent fixation
Sequestering Agent1.0 g/LChelates Ca2+/Mg2+ ions

Component / AuxiliaryDosageFunction

3. Acid Dyeing of Polyamide (Nylon 6 / Nylon 6,6)

Substrate: Nylon Knits | MLR: 1:10 | Temp: 98°C - 100°C

Recipe: Acid Dye 1.5% owf, Ammonium Sulfate 1-2 g/L (pH 4.8 - 5.2), Anionic Leveling Agent 1.0 g/L. Post-treatment: Syntan Fixing Agent 2.5% owf at 70°C for 20 minutes.
