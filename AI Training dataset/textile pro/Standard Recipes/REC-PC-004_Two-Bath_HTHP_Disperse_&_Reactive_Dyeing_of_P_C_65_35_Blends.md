# Standard Dyeing Recipe: REC-PC-004 - Two-Bath HTHP Disperse & Reactive Dyeing of P/C 65/35 Blends

## 1. Basic Information
- **Recipe ID**: REC-PC-004
- **Recipe Name**: Two-Bath HTHP Disperse & Reactive Dyeing of P/C 65/35 Blends
- **Fabric Type**: 65% Polyester / 35% Cotton Woven Twill
- **Fabric GSM**: 240 GSM
- **Fabric Weight**: 400 kg
- **Shade Name**: Forest Green
- **Shade Depth**: Dark (3.5% total owf)
- **End Use**: Workwear & Industrial Uniforms

---

## 2. Dye Information
- **Dye Type**: Disperse Dye (PES component) + Bifunctional Reactive Dye (Cotton component)
- **Dye Name**: Disperse Yellow 54 + Disperse Blue 60 (Step 1) | Reactive Yellow 84 + Reactive Blue 21 (Step 2)
- **Dye Concentration**: Disperse 2.0% owf + Reactive 1.5% owf
- **Dye Combination**: Two-Stage Blend Combination
- **Dye Manufacturer**: Dystar Dianix & Levafix

---

## 3. Chemical Information
| Chemical Name | Function | Dosage | Addition Sequence | Compatibility | Incompatible Chemicals |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dispersing Agent + Acetic Acid (Step 1)** | Protects disperse dyes and maintains pH 4.5 at 130°C | 1.5 g/L Disperser + 1.0 g/L Acetic Acid | Added in initial PES dyeing bath at 50°C | Compatible with disperse auxiliaries | Strong alkalis |
| **Sodium Hydrosulfite + Caustic Soda (Intermediate RC)** | Removes disperse dye stained on cotton fibers before reactive dyeing | 2.0 g/L Hydros + 2.0 g/L Caustic Soda | Intermediate Reduction Clearing bath at 80°C for 20 min | Alkaline reducing bath | Acids |
| **Glauber's Salt + Soda Ash (Step 2)** | Exhaustion electrolyte and alkali fixation for reactive dye on cotton component | 50 g/L Salt + 15 g/L Soda Ash | Dosed in second bath at 60°C | Compatible with reactive dyes | Disperse dyes |

---

## 4. Process Parameters
- **Temperature Profile**: Stage 1 (PES): 60°C -> 130°C @ 1.5°C/min (hold 45m) -> Cool 80°C -> RC @ 80°C -> Stage 2 (Cotton): 60°C Salt/Alkali hold 60m -> Soap @ 95°C
- **Heating Rate**: 1.5 °C/min for Stage 1 | 1.5 °C/min for Stage 2
- **Cooling Rate**: 1.5 °C/min
- **Time**: Total Duration: 360 minutes (6.0 hours total cycle)
- **pH**: Stage 1 pH: 4.5 - 5.0 | RC pH: 11.5 | Stage 2 pH: 10.8 - 11.2
- **Liquor Ratio**: 1:7 MLR
- **Pressure**: Stage 1: 3.0 bar at 130°C | Stage 2: 1.0 bar Atmospheric

---

## 5. Process Steps
- **Pretreatment**: Singeing, desizing, scouring, and Mercerizing (mercerization enhances cotton absorbency)
- **Dye Addition**: Dose Disperse dye at 50°C (Stage 1); Dose Reactive dye at 50°C (Stage 2 after RC)
- **Salt Addition**: Dose 50 g/L Glauber's salt in Stage 2 before alkali addition
- **Alkali Addition**: Dose 15 g/L Soda Ash exponentially over 30 min at 60°C in Stage 2
- **Washing**: Intermediate hot wash after RC; final cold wash after reactive fixation
- **Soaping**: Primary soaping at 95°C for 15 min to remove unfixed reactive dye
- **Neutralization**: Acid neutralize with Acetic Acid (1.0 g/L) at 50°C
- **Drying**: Continuous cylinder dryer followed by Stenter frame at 150°C
- **Finishing**: Apply Durable Press Resin Finish or Softener

- **Machine Capacity**: 500 kg nominal capacity

## 7. Quality Parameters
- **Shade Accuracy**: DE < 0.6 (CMC 2:1)
- **Color Fastness**: Grade 4-5
- **Wash Fastness**: Grade 4-5 (ISO 105-C06 at 60°C)
- **Rub Fastness**: Dry Rubbing: Grade 4-5 | Wet Rubbing: Grade 3-4
- **Light Fastness**: Grade 5 (ISO 105-B02)
- **Uniformity**: Perfect solid shade matching between polyester and cotton components

---

## 8. Defects
- **Common Defects**: Cross-staining / Cross-shade mismatch between PES and Cotton, Low Rub Fastness
- **Causes**: Incomplete reduction clearing of disperse dye off cotton; incorrect dye ratio
- **Corrective Actions**: Ensure thorough RC at 80°C; recalibrate lab match prediction for individual fiber components

---

## 9. Safety
- **PPE Required**: Chemical aprons, face shields, heavy rubber boots, thermal gloves
- **Hazardous Chemicals**: Caustic Soda, Sodium Hydrosulfite, Acetic Acid, Soda Ash
- **Safety Precautions**: High thermal safety precautions during 130°C pressure drain phases
