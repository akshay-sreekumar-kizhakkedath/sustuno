# Standard Industrial Dyeing Recipes & Process Manual

## 1. Introduction
This manual provides standard industrial recipes, chemical formulations, temperature-time profiles, and washing protocols for major textile dye classes across various fiber substrates.

---

## 2. Recipe 1: Exhaust Dyeing of 100% Cotton with Reactive Dyes (Bi-functional / VS Type)

### Substrate: 100% Cotton Knit / Woven
- **Liquor Ratio (MLR)**: 1:8
- **Target Shade Depth**: Medium Shade (2.0% total dye weight)
- **Temperature**: 60°C Isothermal Exhaust Method

### 2.1 Chemical Formulation
| Component / Auxiliary | Concentration / Dosage | Function / Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Reactive Dye (e.g., Reactive Red 195)** | 2.0% owf (on weight of fabric) | Primary Colorant |
| **Glauber's Salt ($Na_2SO_4$) or NaCl** | 45 g/L | Electrolyte to drive dye exhaustion onto fiber |
| **Soda Ash ($Na_2CO_3$)** | 15 g/L | Alkali to induce covalent bond fixation |
| **Sequestering Agent (EDTA/DTPA free)** | 1.0 g/L | Chelates metal ions ($Ca^{2+}, Mg^{2+}$) preventing precipitation |
| **Levelling / Penetrating Agent** | 1.0 g/L | Ensures uniform wetting and liquor distribution |
| **Anti-creasing Agent** | 1.0 – 2.0 g/L | Reduces fabric-to-fabric friction in jet machines |

### 2.2 Salt Requirement by Shade Depth (g/L)
- **Light Shade (< 0.5% owf)**: 15 – 25 g/L Salt | 5 – 8 g/L Soda Ash
- **Medium Shade (0.5% – 2.0% owf)**: 30 – 50 g/L Salt | 10 – 15 g/L Soda Ash
- **Dark Shade (2.0% – 5.0% owf)**: 60 – 80 g/L Salt | 18 – 20 g/L Soda Ash
- **Extra Heavy Black (> 6.0% owf)**: 80 – 100 g/L Salt | 20 – 25 g/L Soda Ash (plus 1-2 g/L Caustic Soda 38°Bé)

### 2.3 Process Profile & Time-Temperature Curve
1. **Wetting & Ramping**: Load fabric at 40°C with Sequestering Agent, Leveling Agent, and Dye. Run for 10 min.
2. **Salt Addition**: Dosing salt over 20 min in two equal splits. Heat to 60°C at 1.5°C/min.
3. **Exhaustion Hold**: Hold at 60°C for 20 min.
4. **Alkali Dosing**: Dose Soda Ash exponentially over 30 min (10% in first 10 min, 30% in next 10 min, 60% in final 10 min).
5. **Fixation Hold**: Hold at 60°C for 45 – 60 min.
6. **Drain & Aftertreatment**:
   - Drain bath $\rightarrow$ Cold Wash (10 min at ambient)
   - Neutralization: Acetic Acid (1.0 g/L) at 50°C for 10 min (target pH 6.0 – 6.5)
   - Hot Wash: 80°C for 10 min
   - Primary Soaping: Polymeric Soaping Agent (1.5 g/L) at 95°C for 15 min (removes unfixed hydrolyzed dye)
   - Secondary Soaping (for dark shades): 95°C for 15 min
   - Final Cold Rinse.

## 3. Recipe 2: HTHP Exhaust Dyeing of 100% Polyester with Disperse Dyes

### Substrate: 100% Polyester (PES) Woven / Knit
- **Liquor Ratio (MLR)**: 1:6
- **Target Shade Depth**: Dark Navy / Black (4.0% owf)
- **Temperature**: 130°C High Temperature High Pressure (HTHP)

### 3.1 Chemical Formulation
| Component / Auxiliary | Concentration / Dosage | Function / Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Disperse Dyes (HT/SE type)** | 4.0% owf | High thermal fastness disperse dyes |
| **Dispersing Agent (Naphthalene Sulfonate)**| 1.5 g/L | Prevents dye agglomeration at elevated temperature |
| **High-Temp Leveling Agent** | 1.0 g/L | Promotes dye migration during 110°C–130°C phase |
| **Acetic Acid (80%)** | 0.8 – 1.2 g/L | Adjusts bath pH to critical range **4.5 – 5.5** |
| **Acid Buffer (Sodium Acetate)** | 0.5 g/L | Maintains stable pH during long thermal cycles |

### 3.2 Reduction Clearing (RC) Recipe (Crucial for Dark Shades)
- **Sodium Hydrosulfite ($Na_2S_2O_4$)**: 2.0 – 3.0 g/L
- **Caustic Soda Flakes ($NaOH$)**: 2.0 g/L
- **Non-ionic Detergent**: 1.0 g/L
- **Conditions**: 80°C for 20 minutes, followed by neutralization with Acetic Acid (1.0 g/L) at 50°C.

### 3.3 Process Profile & Ramping
1. Load bath at 60°C with Acetic Acid, Buffer, Dispersing Agent, Leveling Agent, and Disperse Dyes. Verify pH 4.5–5.0.
2. Heat from 60°C to 90°C at **2.0°C/min**.
3. Heat from 90°C to 130°C at **1.0°C/min** (Slow heating through plasticization strike zone).
4. Hold at 130°C for **45 – 60 minutes** (under ~2.8 bar pressure).
5. Cool from 130°C to 80°C at **1.5°C/min**.
6. Drain and perform Reduction Clearing at 80°C. Neutralize and final rinse.

## 4. Recipe 3: Acid Dyeing of Polyamide (Nylon 6 / Nylon 6,6)

### Substrate: Nylon Knits / Microfiber
- **Liquor Ratio (MLR)**: 1:10
- **Target Shade Depth**: Medium Shade (1.5% owf)
- **Temperature**: 98°C – 100°C

### 4.1 Chemical Formulation
| Component / Auxiliary | Concentration / Dosage | Function / Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Acid Dye (Half-milling or Metal-complex)**| 1.5% owf | Anionic dye for amine end-groups |
| **Ammonium Sulfate / Acetic Acid** | 1.0 – 2.0 g/L | pH sliding agent (pH 4.8 – 5.2) |
| **Anionic Leveling Agent (Retarder)** | 1.0 – 1.5 g/L | Competes for dye sites to prevent streakiness/barre |
| **Syntan Fixing Agent (Post-treatment)** | 2.0 – 3.0% owf | Imparts high wet-fastness and wash-fastness |

### 4.2 Process Profile
1. Set bath at 40°C with Leveling Agent and Ammonium Sulfate (pH 5.5). Run 10 min.
2. Add dissolved Acid Dyes. Run 10 min.
3. Ramp to 98°C at **1.0°C/min**.
4. Hold at 98°C – 100°C for **45 minutes**.
5. Cool to 70°C, drain, rinse.
6. Apply **Syntan After-treatment**: Fresh bath at 70°C, pH 4.5 (Acetic acid), Syntan 2.5% owf for 20 min.

## 5. Recipe 4: Two-Bath Dyeing of Polyester/Cotton (P/C) 65/35 Blend

### 5.1 Step 1: Polyester Component (HTHP Disperse)
- Disperse Dye: As per shade requirement (% owf PES)
- Dispersing Agent: 1.0 g/L, Acetic Acid to pH 4.5.
- Thermal Profile: 60°C $\rightarrow$ 130°C (hold 45 min) $\rightarrow$ Cool to 80°C.
- Intermediate Reduction Clearing (if dark shade).

### 5.2 Step 2: Cotton Component (Reactive Dye)
- Rinse and adjust temperature to 60°C.
- Add Reactive Dye, Glauber's Salt (45 g/L). Run 20 min.
- Dose Soda Ash (15 g/L) over 30 min. Hold at 60°C for 45 min.
- Standard Soap-off sequence (Cold wash $\rightarrow$ Acid neutralize $\rightarrow$ Soap at 95°C $\rightarrow$ Rinse).

## 6. Recipe 5: Continuous Pad-Steam Dyeing of Cotton (Vat Dyes)

### Substrate: 100% Cotton Woven Bottom-weight Fabric
- **Padding Pick-up**: 65% – 70% wet pick-up

### 6.1 Pad Liquors
- **Vat Dye Pigment Padding**: Vat Pigment (30 g/L), Migration Inhibitor (10 g/L), Non-ionic Wetting Agent (2 g/L).
- **Chemical Pad Liquors (Reduction Bath)**:
  - Caustic Soda 38°Bé: 45 mL/L
  - Sodium Hydrosulfite ($Na_2S_2O_4$): 40 g/L
  - Glauber's Salt: 20 g/L

### 6.2 Process Sequence
1. **Pad Dye Pigment** at ambient temperature $\rightarrow$ Infrared Pre-dryer (reduce moisture to 25%) $\rightarrow$ Hot Air Dryer.
2. **Pad Chemical Reduction Bath** in chemical padder.
3. **Steam Treatment**: Pass through saturated steamer at **102°C – 104°C** for 45 – 60 seconds (no air ingress).
4. **Oxidation Wash-off**:
   - Cold Water Rinse.
   - Oxidation Bath: Hydrogen Peroxide (35%) 3.0 mL/L + Acetic Acid 1.0 g/L at 60°C.
   - Hot Soaping at 95°C for 5 minutes $\rightarrow$ Final Drying.
