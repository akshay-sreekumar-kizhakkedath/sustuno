# Standard Dyeing Recipe: REC-PES-002 - HTHP Exhaust Dyeing of 100% Polyester Woven Fabric with Disperse Dyes

## 1. Basic Information
- **Recipe ID**: REC-PES-002
- **Recipe Name**: HTHP Exhaust Dyeing of 100% Polyester Woven Fabric with Disperse Dyes
- **Fabric Type**: 100% Polyester Microfiber Woven Fabric
- **Fabric GSM**: 140 GSM
- **Fabric Weight**: 300 kg
- **Shade Name**: Dark Navy Blue
- **Shade Depth**: Dark (4.0% owf)
- **End Use**: Activewear / Sportswear Jackets

---

## 2. Dye Information
- **Dye Type**: High-Thermal Fastness Disperse Dye (SE / HW Type)
- **Dye Name**: Disperse Blue 79 (2.8% owf) + Disperse Navy 56 (1.0% owf) + Disperse Rubine 56 (0.2% owf)
- **Dye Concentration**: 4.0% owf total dye solids
- **Dye Combination**: Trichromatic Disperse Combination
- **Dye Manufacturer**: Dystar Dianix / Huntsman Teratop

---

## 3. Chemical Information
| Chemical Name | Function | Dosage | Addition Sequence | Compatibility | Incompatible Chemicals |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Naphthalene Sulfonate Dispersing Agent** | Keeps non-ionic disperse dye particles suspended in water at 130°C | 1.5 g/L | Added to dye bath at 50°C before dye dispersion addition | Compatible with non-ionic and anionic leveling agents | Cationic surfactants |
| **Acetic Acid (80%) + Sodium Acetate Buffer** | Maintains acidic bath pH to prevent disperse dye reduction | 1.0 g/L Acetic Acid + 0.5 g/L Sodium Acetate | Dosed during bath setup to establish target pH 4.5 - 5.0 | Compatible with all disperse dye auxiliaries | Alkalis |

---

## 4. Process Parameters
- **Temperature Profile**: Start 60°C -> Heat to 90°C @ 2.0°C/min -> Heat to 130°C @ 1.0°C/min -> Hold 130°C for 45 min -> Cool to 80°C @ 1.5°C/min
- **Heating Rate**: 1.0 °C/min through critical plasticization strike zone (90°C to 130°C)
- **Cooling Rate**: 1.5 °C/min (controlled cooling to prevent oligomer precipitation)
- **Time**: Total Duration: 180 minutes (3.0 hours)
- **pH**: Dyeing pH: 4.5 - 5.0 | Reduction Clearing pH: 11.5 - 12.0
- **Liquor Ratio**: 1:6 MLR
- **Pressure**: 2.8 to 3.2 bar pressurized vessel at 130°C

---

## 5. Process Steps
- **Pretreatment**: Preshrunk and heat-set woven polyester; free of silicone knitting oils
- **Dye Addition**: Disperse dyes pasted with warm water (50°C), high-shear mixed, filtered into tank
- **Salt Addition**: N/A (Electrolytes not used in disperse dyeing)
- **Alkali Addition**: N/A during dyeing bath; Caustic Soda added during Reduction Clearing
- **Washing**: Drain 130°C bath at 80°C -> Hot rinse at 70°C
- **Soaping**: Reduction Clearing: 2.5 g/L Hydros + 2.0 g/L NaOH + 1 g/L detergent at 80°C for 20 min
- **Neutralization**: Drain RC bath -> Rinse -> Neutralize with 1.0 g/L Acetic Acid at 50°C for 10 min
- **Drying**: Stenter frame drying and final heat-setting at 170°C for 30 seconds
- **Finishing**: Apply 1.0% Hydrophilic moisture-wicking finish

## 7. Quality Parameters
- **Shade Accuracy**: DE < 0.5 (CMC 2:1)
- **Color Fastness**: Grade 4-5
- **Wash Fastness**: Grade 4-5 (ISO 105-C06)
- **Rub Fastness**: Dry Rubbing: Grade 4-5 | Wet Rubbing: Grade 4
- **Light Fastness**: Grade 5-6
- **Uniformity**: Completely streak-free and oligomer-free fabric surface

---

## 8. Defects
- **Common Defects**: Oligomer White Powder Spotting, Color Specks, Thermal Translocation Unlevelness
- **Causes**: Rapid cooling below 100°C, dye agglomeration, unstable pH > 5.5 during dyeing
- **Corrective Actions**: Hot drain at 80°C; add high-temp dispersing agent; maintain pH strictly at 4.5

---

## 9. Safety
- **PPE Required**: Heat-resistant safety gloves, full face shield, protective chemical suit during sampling
- **Hazardous Chemicals**: Sodium Hydrosulfite (spontaneously combustible when damp), Caustic Soda (corrosive)
- **Safety Precautions**: Ensure vessel pressure gauge reads 0.0 bar before unlocking pneumatic door safety pin
