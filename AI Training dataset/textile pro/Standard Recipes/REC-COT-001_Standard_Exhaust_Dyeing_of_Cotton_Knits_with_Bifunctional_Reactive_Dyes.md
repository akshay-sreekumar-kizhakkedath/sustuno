# Standard Dyeing Recipe: REC-COT-001 - Standard Exhaust Dyeing of Cotton Knits with Bifunctional Reactive Dyes

## 1. Basic Information
- **Recipe ID**: REC-COT-001
- **Recipe Name**: Standard Exhaust Dyeing of Cotton Knits with Bifunctional Reactive Dyes
- **Fabric Type**: 100% Cotton Single Jersey Knit
- **Fabric GSM**: 180 GSM
- **Fabric Weight**: 250 kg
- **Shade Name**: Royal Blue
- **Shade Depth**: Medium (2.0% owf)
- **End Use**: Apparel / T-Shirts

---

## 2. Dye Information
- **Dye Type**: Bifunctional Reactive Dye (VS / MCT Chemistry)
- **Dye Name**: Reactive Blue 19 (1.2% owf) + Reactive Red 195 (0.5% owf) + Reactive Yellow 145 (0.3% owf)
- **Dye Concentration**: 2.0% owf total dye solids
- **Dye Combination**: Trichromatic Combination (Blue/Red/Yellow)
- **Dye Manufacturer**: Dystar / Huntsman Chemical

---

## 3. Chemical Information
| Chemical Name | Function | Dosage | Addition Sequence | Compatibility | Incompatible Chemicals |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Glauber's Salt (Sodium Sulfate Na2SO4)** | Electrolyte to drive dye exhaustion onto fiber surface | 45 g/L | Added in 2 equal splits at 40°C prior to alkali dosing | Compatible with non-ionic and anionic wetting agents | Cationic fixing agents, strong concentrated acids |
| **Soda Ash (Sodium Carbonate Na2CO3)** | Alkali buffer to generate cellulosate ions for covalent dye fixation | 15 g/L | Dosed exponentially over 30 min after 20 min salt exhaustion hold | Compatible with standard reactive dye auxiliaries | Direct acids, oxidizing agents |

---

## 4. Process Parameters
- **Temperature Profile**: Start 40°C -> Heat to 60°C @ 1.5°C/min -> Hold 60 min -> Cool to 50°C @ 2.0°C/min -> Soap @ 95°C
- **Heating Rate**: 1.5 °C/min
- **Cooling Rate**: 2.0 °C/min
- **Time**: Total Duration: 210 minutes (3.5 hours)
- **pH**: Exhaustion pH: 6.8 - 7.2 | Fixation pH: 10.8 - 11.2 | Neutralized pH: 6.0 - 6.5
- **Liquor Ratio**: 1:8 MLR
- **Pressure**: 1.0 bar (Atmospheric)

---

## 5. Process Steps
- **Pretreatment**: Scoured & bleached cotton; core pH 6.5; residual H2O2 < 5 ppm
- **Dye Addition**: Dissolve dyes in warm water (50°C), strain through mesh, dose over 10 min at 40°C
- **Salt Addition**: Dose Glauber's salt (45 g/L) in two portions (50% at 40°C, 50% at 60°C)
- **Alkali Addition**: Dose Soda Ash (15 g/L) exponentially over 30 min once 60°C is reached
- **Washing**: Drain fixation bath -> Cold rinse (10 min) -> Hot rinse at 70°C (10 min)
- **Soaping**: Neutralize with Acetic Acid (1.0 g/L) -> Soap with Polymeric Agent at 95°C for 15 min
- **Neutralization**: Final rinse with Acetic Acid (0.5 g/L) to bring core fabric pH to 6.0 - 6.5
- **Drying**: Relaxation dryer or Stenter frame at 120°C to target 6% moisture
- **Finishing**: Apply 1.5% Bio-softener via padding or exhaust at 40°C

## 7. Quality Parameters
- **Shade Accuracy**: DE < 0.6 (CMC 2:1 against standard master swatch)
- **Color Fastness**: Grade 4-5
- **Wash Fastness**: Grade 4-5 (ISO 105-C06 at 60°C)
- **Rub Fastness**: Dry Rubbing: Grade 4-5 | Wet Rubbing: Grade 3-4
- **Light Fastness**: Grade 4 (ISO 105-B02)
- **Uniformity**: Zero end-to-end or streakiness unlevelness

---

## 8. Defects
- **Common Defects**: Patchiness / Unlevelness, Crease Marks, Low Wet Fastness
- **Causes**: Rapid alkali addition, turn time > 120 s, incomplete removal of hydrolyzed dye
- **Corrective Actions**: Leveling agent strip & redye; optimize exponential alkali dosing; ensure soaping at 95°C

---

## 9. Safety
- **PPE Required**: Chemical splash goggles, neoprene gloves, rubber apron, dust mask during dye powder weighing
- **Hazardous Chemicals**: Sodium Carbonate (irritant), Glacial Acetic Acid (corrosive)
- **Safety Precautions**: Operate eye wash station nearby; store acids and alkalis in separate bunded areas
